# Example FAQ

Replace this file with your own content — product docs, FAQs, policies,
pricing, anything you want the bot to answer from. Drop as many .txt or .md
files as you like anywhere under this `knowledge/` folder (subfolders are
fine too), then restart the server or hit `POST /knowledge/reload`.

Keep each fact as its own paragraph (separated by a blank line) — the bot
retrieves the most relevant paragraphs for each question, so one topic per
paragraph works better than one giant wall of text.

## What are your support hours?

Our support team is available Monday to Friday, 9am-6pm IST. Outside those
hours, the chatbot can still answer common questions, and anything urgent
gets escalated to a human the next business day.

## What is your refund policy?

We offer a full refund within 14 days of purchase, no questions asked.
After 14 days, refunds are considered case-by-case — contact support with
your order number.

## How do I reset my password?

Go to the login page and click "Forgot password". You'll get an email with
a reset link that's valid for 30 minutes.
